Posel

A native mail app for macOS.

Posel reads and sends e‑mail for Gmail, IMAP and POP3 accounts. It shows mail as conversations, lets you write replies with signatures and attachments, and keeps drafts in sync with the server.

Gmail

For Gmail, Posel can sign in with your Google account. It uses that sign-in only to connect to Gmail over IMAP and SMTP from your Mac: to download your mail, send the messages you write, and keep folders, drafts and read status in sync. Posel has no server of its own; your mail goes only between your Mac and Google.

Posel is a personal project, not available for download.
