Privacy Policy

Posel, a mail app for macOS. Last updated 28 October 2026.

What Posel accesses

When you sign in with Google, Posel asks for access to Gmail (https://mail.google.com/) and your e‑mail address. It uses it to download your messages and folders, send the messages you write, and change messages as you tell it to (read status, flags, moving, deleting, drafts).

Where your data goes

Posel runs only on your Mac and has no server of its own. Messages, attachments and settings are stored on your Mac. The sign-in is kept in the macOS keychain. Posel connects only to Google's mail servers (and to other mail servers you add yourself). It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't sell or share your data with anyone.

Claude

Posel can let the Claude apps on your Mac read and draft mail for you. This is off until you turn it on in Posel's settings. When it's on, the mail Claude asks for goes to the Claude app you're using and is handled under Anthropic's privacy policy.

Google user data

Posel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Removing an account in Posel deletes its downloaded mail and sign-in from your Mac; your mail on Google's servers stays. You can also end Posel's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
